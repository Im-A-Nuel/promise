# antislop audit 001, 2026-11-12

Mode: AFTER (audit of the finished app). Target: https://promise-near.vercel.app after PR #9 and #10. Skills: antislop 3.2.28 (core, ui, human, layoutmobile).
Design Read: wedding gift ledger for Indonesian users and hackathon judges, soft 3D warm style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 (from DESIGN.md).

Nothing was changed. Pick the numbers to fix.

## Measured, not guessed
- Text contrast: 0 failures on `/`, `/events`, `/events/1`, `/demo`, `/ledger` (script computes WCAG ratio over the real background).
- Horizontal overflow at 390 px: none on 6 routes.
- Images without alt: 0. `<html lang>` set. No control without an accessible name.
- Not exercised: form submits and the wallet flow (no click-through of transactions in this audit).

## Hard Gate (HIGH)
1. **R-39 pointer cursor.** Buttons keep the arrow cursor: the EN/ID toggle, "Use Ayu/Budi/Citra/Dewi/Eko", every "Open as ...", the carousel dots. Fix: one `cursor: pointer` rule in the base layer (disabled gets `not-allowed`).
2. **R-39 unique title.** `/events/1` has the title "Promise", same as the home page. Fix: event title in `<title>`.
3. **R-03 tap target.** Carousel dots on the home page are 36 x 44 px. Fix: width 44.

## Purpose-Gate (MEDIUM)
4. **R-20, R-22, C-1: two envelope styles.** Glossy SVG Pao (hero, "four states", final CTA, empty states, error, toasts) and the new 3D clay envelope (event guest cards) show the same character twice in different looks. Pixel art stays: owner decision, it is the identity motif for people, coins and records (story steps, "Limits" icons, video). Rule that makes the three styles one system: the envelope (Pao) is 3D, people and money are pixel. Recommended: use the clay poses wherever the envelope appears.
5. **R-20: people are initials.** Five demo personas are a letter in a circle (Accounts page, switcher, event header, guest rows). The Accounts grid also ends with one empty slot. Owner point: avatars must work for any user, not only the five demo personas. Fix without any generated image: a pixel avatar built in code from the account id (same account always gets the same look): skin tone, hair style and color, shirt color, and an optional accessory (peci, hijab, glasses, bun) layered on the existing `PERSON` sprite. The five demo personas keep their curated looks (Ayu and Budi already have palettes). No stored profile, so no database. Clay avatars per persona are dropped.
6. **R-14: five identical "Limits" cards.** Same size, same pixel icon treatment. Acceptable because the five limits have equal weight (reason written). Pixel icons stay.

## Quality Locks (LOW)
7. **Share image.** `opengraph-image.tsx` is text plus a small brand mark. The X post and link preview are what judges see first. Reusing the clay "open with coin" pose as the picture needs no new asset.
8. **Event kind icons** (6 thin line glyphs in 56 px tiles) are the only line-icon set left in the app. Optional.

## Checked and passing
- Hero "Connected" pill above the H1: marks a real state (RPC health), not a label the headline repeats. PASS while it stays tied to `/api/health`.
- No gradients on the page background beyond the brand peach, no bento, no fake stats, no testimonials, no 3-step "how it works" (the story is the real 3-step ledger flow, with real amounts).
- Mobile first screen shows the demo button (PR #9).
